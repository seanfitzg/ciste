# Ciste Fees and Terms

These are the Fees Ciste applies to an Account and the terms around them. Each clause has an ID (for example FT-3.2) so support can cite it.

## 1. About these terms

- **FT-1.1** These terms apply to every Customer and every Account. Ciste is a fictional Irish neobank, and nothing here describes a real product.
- **FT-1.2** A Fee is a charge Ciste itself applies to an Account. A charge raised by a third-party merchant is a Merchant charge, not a Fee, and is not covered by these terms.
- **FT-1.3** Amounts are in euro (€) and are charged to the Account when the event happens.

## 2. Account and Tiers

- **FT-2.1** Each Customer holds one Account and has a Tier, either retail or premium. The Tier belongs to the Customer, not the Account.
- **FT-2.2** There is no monthly maintenance Fee on a retail or premium Account.
- **FT-2.3** A Customer's Tier decides the Refund cap in section 7. It does not change the amount of any Fee.

## 3. Overdraft Fees

- **FT-3.1** An Account may not go overdrawn unless the Customer has agreed an overdraft limit with Ciste.
- **FT-3.2** If a payment takes the Account below zero without an agreed overdraft, Ciste applies an unarranged overdraft Fee of **€65**, once per calendar month.
- **FT-3.3** Spending beyond an agreed overdraft limit is charged as in FT-3.2.

## 4. Card Fees

- **FT-4.1** A Card is replaced free of charge if Ciste issues the new Card because of expiry or a manufacturing fault.
- **FT-4.2** A card replacement Fee of **€10** applies when a Customer asks for a replacement of a lost, stolen or damaged Card.
- **FT-4.3** An express delivery Fee of **€25** applies when a Customer asks for a replacement Card to arrive within two working days. It is charged in addition to FT-4.2.
- **FT-4.4** Freezing and unfreezing a Card is always free.

## 5. International transfers

- **FT-5.1** An international transfer to an account outside the euro area carries a Fee of **€15** per transfer.
- **FT-5.2** If a Customer asks Ciste to recall an international transfer after it has been sent, a recall Fee of **€175** applies, whether or not the recall succeeds.
- **FT-5.3** Transfers within the euro area are free.

## 6. Returned payments

- **FT-6.1** When a direct debit or standing order cannot be paid because the Account has too little money, Ciste applies a returned payment Fee of **€12** per payment.
- **FT-6.2** No more than three returned payment Fees are applied in one calendar month.

## 7. Refunds of Fees

- **FT-7.1** Ciste may Refund a Fee it has applied. A Refund reverses one of Ciste's own Fees and never applies to a Merchant charge.
- **FT-7.2** A single Refund may not exceed the Refund cap for the Customer's Tier: **€50** for retail and **€150** for premium.
- **FT-7.3** A Fee can be refunded at most once.
- **FT-7.4** A Fee larger than the Refund cap for the Customer's Tier cannot be refunded by support. The Customer can ask for an Escalation.
- **FT-7.5** Every Refund within the cap is proposed to a Reviewer, who approves, edits or rejects it before it is issued.

## 8. Important information

- **FT-8.1** This document describes Ciste's Fees and terms. It is not financial, legal or tax advice, and support will not recommend which products or actions suit a Customer's circumstances.
- **FT-8.2** If a Customer needs advice, they should speak to a qualified, independent adviser.
- **FT-8.3** Ciste may change these terms. The version in force on the day a Fee is applied governs that Fee.
